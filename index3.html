How You Can Help

← Back to Home

Everyone has a role to play in keeping our communities clean. Here are simple and effective ways you can make a difference starting today.

🗑️ Dispose Responsibly

Always throw trash in designated bins. If you can't find a bin, hold onto your waste until you do. Never leave garbage behind — even small items like gum or cigarette butts matter.

♻️ Recycle and Reduce Waste

Sort your waste properly and recycle whenever possible. Reduce the use of single-use plastics by choosing reusable bags, bottles, and containers.

🌎 Join Community Cleanups

Participate in local cleanup events or start your own with friends, neighbors, or classmates. Every piece picked up is a step toward a cleaner planet.

🗣️ Educate and Inspire

Talk to friends and family about littering and its effects. Share tips and information on social media to spread awareness in your community.
