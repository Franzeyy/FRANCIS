Why Reducing Litter Matters

Litter harms wildlife, pollutes our environment, and makes our communities less enjoyable. Everyone can make a difference by properly disposing of waste and encouraging others to do the same.

How You Can Help

  • Always use trash and recycling bins
  • Join local clean-up events
  • Educate friends and family
  • Reduce single-use plastics
