Why Reducing Littering Matters

← Back to Home

Littering affects every part of our world — from the oceans to our neighborhoods. Here's why it's everyone's responsibility to take action.

🌿 Environmental Impact

Litter, especially plastics, pollutes our land and waterways. Animals often ingest it or become trapped, leading to injury or death. A clean environment supports healthy ecosystems and wildlife.

🧍 Human Health & Safety

Discarded waste can harbor bacteria and attract disease-carrying pests. Sharp objects and chemicals left in public areas are a danger to people, particularly children.

🏙️ Economic and Social Costs

Cleaning up litter drains public funds that could be used elsewhere. Litter also decreases property values and discourages community pride. Cleaner spaces encourage tourism, business, and well-being.
